Brewlo

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Brewlo (“the App”) handles information. Brewlo is a personal pour-over coffee journal for iPhone and iPad. It does not operate a Brewlo cloud account backend.

1. Data location

Brew records, bean lots, recipes, tasting notes, attached photos, credit balance, and local guest session identifiers are stored in the App’s on-device sandbox (Application Support). We do not operate a Brewlo server that receives or syncs this content. Uninstalling the App or losing an unbacked-up device permanently removes local data unless you previously exported files yourself.

2. Account and session

Sign-in uses one-tap local guest login. The App does not offer Sign in with Apple, email/password registration, or third-party social login. A local guest identifier is stored on device so the App can keep you signed in across launches. Log Out clears the local session and returns you to the login screen while leaving journal content on device. Delete Account removes the local guest identity together with journal data, photos stored by the App, and local credits, then returns you to the login screen. The next one-tap login creates a new local guest experience.

3. Permissions

  • Camera. Requested only when you tap Take Photo to attach a coffee bag or brew reference image. Photos are saved locally with the brew record.
  • Photo library. The App uses the system photo picker (PhotosUI). It does not request full photo-library access.

The App does not request microphone, speech recognition, notifications, or App Tracking Transparency (IDFA) access.

4. In-app purchases

Optional consumable credit packs are sold through Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Apple processes payment. Credits are tracked in the local wallet ledger. Consumable credits are not restored through a Restore Purchases flow. Refunds and billing disputes are handled by Apple under Apple’s terms.

5. Starter content

Bean lots, recipes, or brew entries shown after install are local starter data stored only on your device. They are not a live networked social feed and are not shared with other users through Brewlo.

6. Exports

If you use Export, the App can create JSON, CSV, and optional media files that you save or share through the system share sheet. Once those files leave the App, you are responsible for how they are stored or shared.

7. Analytics and advertising

Brewlo does not include third-party analytics SDKs or advertising networks that collect personal data for cross-app tracking.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Do not use Brewlo if you are under the age required by your jurisdiction for such apps.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The Effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy are handled through in-app Support in Brewlo Settings. There is no separate web support form and no public contact email on this site.
